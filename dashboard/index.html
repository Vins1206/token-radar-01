How to read this board

Peer multiple is the median market-cap/TVL of token-bearing protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global median with weight n/(n+8) and clamped to 0.03–2.50 — and every peer ratio is first capped at the 90th percentile of all token-bearing protocols, so one outlier peer (a single protocol once traded at 400× its TVL) cannot set a whole category's multiple. Growth adds 40% of the clamped seven-day TVL change (−50% to +100%), so a fast grower can outrank a stagnant giant. Maturity runs from 0.72 to 1.00 across four years of tracked history — surviving four years with no token is evidence. Breadth adds 1.5% per extra chain, up to six. Readiness (0–100) is a separate triage score: scale, track record, momentum, breadth.

The exit rule is part of every plan: leave any farm whose TVL falls 30% from your entry week, and if a token launches, claim on day one and sell at least half into the first week. A radar that never tells you to leave is a trap, not a tool.
