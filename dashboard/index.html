How to read this board

How an implied value is made, in four steps. (1) Peer multiple: take every token-bearing protocol in the same category, divide its market cap by its TVL, cap each ratio at the 90th percentile of all token-bearing protocols (2.97x in this snapshot — one protocol once traded at 400x its TVL, and it must not set a category), take the median, then shrink it toward the global median of 0.21x with weight n/(n+8), and clamp the result to 0.03–2.50. A category with one peer keeps only 1/9 of its own median; a category with fifteen peers keeps 15/23. (2) Growth: add 40% of the clamped seven-day TVL change (−50% to +100%), so a fast grower can outrank a stagnant giant. (3) Maturity: 0.72 to 1.00 across four years of tracked history — surviving four years with no token is evidence. (4) Breadth: 1.5% per extra chain, up to six. Multiply the four: implied value = TVL × peer × growth × maturity × breadth. Readiness (0–100) is a separate triage score: scale, track record, momentum, breadth. Farm plans are written per protocol, not per category: six steps that name the protocol's own TVL, seven-day momentum, tracked age, chains and readiness, with an eight-week timeline and the same 30% exit trigger.

Worked example — rank #1, Steakhouse Financial. TVL $2.75B × peer 0.52 (Risk Curators: one token-bearing peer, its ratio capped at 2.97x, then shrunk 8/9 of the way toward the global 0.21x) × growth 0.99 (TVL −2.2% in seven days) × maturity 0.82 × breadth 1.09 (three chains) = $1.28B implied. Every factor is printed on the protocol's own board row and detail view, so the arithmetic can be checked without trusting this paragraph.

What this board does not claim. Risk Curators' 0.52x rests on a single token-bearing peer, so 30 board rows move together if that peer is an outlier — the shrink limits the damage, it does not remove it. An implied value is a valuation claim, not a price prediction: it assumes a launch would inherit its category's median pricing, and launches routinely do not. “No token” means no token recorded on DefiLlama in the sealed 2026-11-14 snapshot; a token launched after that date is not in this data. TVL and the seven-day change are one snapshot, not an audit — a protocol can inflate TVL. Readiness is a triage score, not a return forecast.

The exit rule is part of every plan: leave any farm whose TVL falls 30% from your entry week, and if a token launches, claim on day one and sell at least half into the first week. A radar that never tells you to leave is a trap, not a tool.
